PRIVACY POLICY

Profiles are unverified

SAINDHAV ARENA's email sign-in is profile selection, not verified authentication. You type an email address and the site opens the profile stored under that address on this device. Nobody proves they own the email, so an email here is never treated as proof of identity, and it never unlocks private information or sensitive account actions.

What is stored, and where

There is no account database and no server storing your data. Your profile — recently played games, favourite games, best scores, last-played dates, and your sound setting — is stored only in this browser's local storage on this device, kept separate for each email address used on this device. It is not synced across devices, and there is no cross-device secure account.

No passwords

This system uses no passwords. No password is asked for, stored, or compared, and no verification codes or emails are sent.

Guest play

You can play every game as a guest without entering an email. Guest data is stored on this device in the same way, under a guest profile.

Your control

Signing out or switching email does not delete a profile — it just stops using it on this device, so the same email can reopen it later on this device. To remove everything, delete all profiles stored on this device:

You can also clear this site's data at any time from your browser settings, which removes the same stored profiles.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Free browser games

SAINDHAV ARENA provides free games that run in your browser. No purchase is required to play, and no account is mandatory — guest play is always available.

Virtual points only

Profiles

Playing together
